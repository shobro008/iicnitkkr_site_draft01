About Us

The Indian Institute of Technology (Aligarh Muslim University), Aligarh, is a premier institution under the Ministry of Education, Government of India. Integrating the historic legacy of AMU with modern technological excellence, the institute fosters innovation, academic research, and leadership in science and engineering.

Academics

  • Undergraduate Programs in Engineering and Technology
  • Postgraduate and Doctoral Research Opportunities
  • Advanced Laboratories and Innovation Centers
  • Global Collaborations and Exchange Programs

Admissions

Admission to IIT (AMU), Aligarh is through national-level competitive exams. B.Tech aspirants must qualify JEE Advanced, while M.Tech admissions require GATE scores. The institute seeks exceptional students committed to academic and research excellence.
